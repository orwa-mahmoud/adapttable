New Vue kit, `@adapttable/ant-design-vue`: the AdaptTable data table drawn with Ant Design Vue 4 components, with the same 41 canonical feature factories, public parts and focused entry points as the other Vue kits. Menus gain arrow-key navigation and the filter drawer and assistant sheet gain a focus trap, because Ant Design Vue's `Menu` and `Drawer` do not provide them. The CLI detects `ant-design-vue` and scaffolds this kit, and the Vue showcase has its landing, feature pages and order desk.
