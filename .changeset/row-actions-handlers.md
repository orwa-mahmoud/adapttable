`rowActions` accepts add, duplicate and delete handlers: `rowActions(actions, { onAddRow, onDuplicateRow, onDeleteRow, confirmDeleteRow })` puts Add row in the toolbar and Duplicate / Delete on every row. Every `/row-actions` entry also exports the `RowMutationHandlers` type.
