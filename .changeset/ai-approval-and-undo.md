An assistant turn that moved the table can be put back: the view is captured
before the turn runs and restored through the same capabilities, and the offer
ends the moment anything else changes the view. A backend can put a structured
question to the reader mid-turn and resume when they answer.
`approval.alwaysAllow` names the capabilities a reader may wave through — off
unless you name them, never for a destructive one, and revocable.
