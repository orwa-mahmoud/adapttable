`commandPalette({ button: true })` adds a toolbar control that opens the palette, and `commandPalette({ open, onOpenChange })` lets a host open it from its own control. The context menu offers Pin to top, Pin to bottom and Unpin on rows when `rowPinning()` is composed, and Cut on cells when `cellNavigation()` and `onCellCut` are both wired. Unstyled and shadcn style the new control through `classNames.commandPaletteButton`.
