Every React kit draws its desktop table and phone cards through one shared structure, `DesktopTableChrome` and `MobileCardsChrome`. Each kit still supplies its own table, rows, cells, cards and controls, and every table looks and behaves as before. `mobileCardSlots` builds the card list (order, pinned totals, groups, extra rows and each card's list-item attributes) and `mobileCardPropsEqual` is its card body's memo comparator, so a kit draws only its card host and body. A card now re-renders when the host passes a new `renderCard`, and a card offers no expand toggle without `renderRowDetail`, as the desktop table does.

A plain React table no longer loads the editing session or the extra-row splicer: enter-to-edit and batch undo come with the cell-navigation and editing features, and extra rows are spliced only when `extraRows()` is composed.

The MUI filters panel stays within the window below its trigger after the page shifts while it is open, and inside its left edge in a right-to-left page with a scrollbar.

A React kit can hand the binding one typed set of primitives (`KitPrimitives`: button, icon button, text input, select, checkbox, popover, menu, text) and get each feature's controls built from them: `createFindBar`, `createRowEditActions`, `createBatchEditBar`, `createAgentApproval`, `createTreeToggle`, `createTreeCell`, `createColumnGroupToggle`, `createGroupMoreButton`, `createRowReorderHandle`, `createRowReorderButtons`, `createEditableCellSlots`, `createFilterHeaderRow` and `createFilterHeaderControl`, all from `@adapttable/react/adapter`. `TableComposition` draws the whole table from a kit's `KitTable`: the kit supplies a frame that lays the regions out, its toolbar, body, state views and pager, and the binding wires everything else. The MUI kit draws its table and these controls this way; its header multi-choice filter carries `filter-header-menu` on the panel that takes `classNames.filterHeaderMenu`, as the other kits do. `createColumnMenu(kit, useLook)` builds the Columns menu the same way, with the kit's spacing in a `KitColumnMenuLook`; MUI's Columns menu uses it, Mantine draws its table, feature controls and Columns menu the same way (keeping its own row-move menu through the factories' `MoveMenu`), and in a right-to-left page its rows, bulk buttons and rename buttons are spaced as the mirror of left-to-right. A kit whose text input draws its own label and error passes a `RenameField` to `createColumnMenu`. Escape in the Columns menu puts focus back on its button.

`createDesktopTable(kit)` draws the desktop table's rows, rails, header cells, spacers and column summary from a kit's table elements and paint; MUI's and Mantine's desktop tables use it, and `createMobileCards(kit)` does the same for the phone cards: each card's controls, fields, actions and detail, arranged by the kit's own frame. `createToolbar(kit)` and `createGroupHeader(kit)` build the toolbar and the group rows and cards the same way for MUI and Mantine. The toolbar controls the features add (undo and redo, CSV export, print, density, fullscreen, the command palette and find) each come from their own factory, and a primitive button marked `busy` shows the kit's working state. `createRowActionButtons(kit)` decides a row's actions once (which show, why one is disabled, confirm, strip or menu) for MUI and Mantine. `createCellEditor(kit)` and `createEditableCell(kit)` pick and wire the in-place editor once; each kit gives its checkbox, selects and input. `createBulkBar(kit)` decides the bulk action bar once: Mantine's scope offer now counts the rows on the page, and MUI marks the scope offer as `select-all-banner` only while it is offered, as every other kit does; the offer is a `status` region in both. Chakra builds its find bar, header filters, row edit, batch, approval, tree, column-group, show-more, reorder and editable-cell controls through the binding from its own primitives. Chakra's row actions come from `createRowActionButtons`, and `accentColor` reaches every Chakra control from the table root. Chakra's cell editor and bulk action bar come from the binding too; its scope offer is a `status` region. Chakra's toolbar feature controls (undo and redo, export, print, density, fullscreen, palette, find) come from the binding's per-control factories. Chakra's toolbar is laid out by the binding's `createToolbar`. Chakra's desktop table is drawn by `createDesktopTable`; a kit's paint can shape a column heading's caption row with `captionRow`. Chakra's phone cards come from `createMobileCards`, whose field look now also receives whether the list is compact. Chakra's group rows and cards come from `createGroupHeader`. Chakra's Columns menu comes from `createColumnMenu` and holds its rows in a group named for the menu, as the other kits' menus do. Chakra's table is laid out by `TableComposition` in its own bordered frame. `createColumnHeaderRename(kit, useLook)` builds a column header's rename button and form on the same rename form the Columns menu opens; a kit's `RenameField` receives the part names for wherever the form opens (`KitRenameFieldParts`). MUI, Mantine and Chakra draw their header rename this way. `createSavedViewsMenu(kit, useLook)` builds the saved-views menu: applying a view closes it, deleting one keeps it open, the rule under the list shows only while there is a list, Escape closes it from anywhere inside and returns focus to its button, and after saving the name field keeps focus for the next name. MUI, Mantine and Chakra draw their saved-views menu this way, and its button, panel, rows, items, delete buttons, save row, field and save button carry `views-*` parts as the unstyled kit's do. `createPager(kit)` builds the table footer (page size, range, "page n of m" and the page controls) from a kit's frame and pieces, and `createPageButtons(kit, look)` builds windowed page buttons for a kit without its own pagination component; MUI and Mantine keep their pagination components, and Chakra's page buttons carry the `page-*` parts the unstyled kit's do. Base UI builds its find bar, header filters, row edit, batch, approval, tree, column-group, show-more, reorder and editable-cell controls through the binding from its own primitives, and its touch-target context is the binding's. Base UI's row actions, cell editor, bulk action bar and toolbar feature controls come from the binding too, and `accentColor` reaches its controls from the table root. Base UI's toolbar is laid out by `createToolbar` and its desktop table drawn by `createDesktopTable`. Its phone cards come from `createMobileCards` and its group rows and cards from `createGroupHeader`. Its Columns menu and header rename come from `createColumnMenu` and `createColumnHeaderRename`. In every kit, Enter in a rename field saves from the field itself. A kit's Columns menu look may space its fixed rows apart (`fixedRow`), and its header rename may put the button before the caption (`buttonFirst`). Base UI's saved-views menu, pager and table frame come from `createSavedViewsMenu`, `createPager` and `TableComposition`, and its context menu opens inside the fullscreen table from its own `container`. Radix builds its small controls through the binding from its own primitives, and its touch-target context is the binding's. Radix's row actions, cell editor, bulk action bar, toolbar feature controls and toolbar come from the binding too, and `accentColor` reaches its controls from the table root. Radix's desktop table and phone cards come from `createDesktopTable` and `createMobileCards`; a kit's paint may give pinned totals its own weight (`summaryCell`). Radix's group rows, Columns menu, header rename, saved-views menu, pager and table frame come from the binding as well, and its context menu and Columns menu open inside a fullscreen table. A kit whose popover scrolls may keep the Columns and saved-views panels from scrolling too (`panel.overflowY`). antd builds its small controls through the binding from its own primitives, and its touch-target context is the binding's. antd's row actions, cell editor, bulk action bar (its `Alert`), toolbar feature controls and toolbar come from the binding too. The header filter factories take a kit's own multiple choice (`Multi`, in a `KitFilterHeader`) in place of the shared popover one. The unstyled kit, and shadcn and Tailwind on it, build their small controls through the binding from native elements, keeping their disclosure-based multiple choice and move menu, and share the binding's touch-target context. The unstyled kit's row actions, cell editor, bulk action bar and toolbar feature controls come from the binding too, each control taking the class of its part's `classNames` key. Its toolbar is laid out by `createToolbar`. Its desktop table is drawn by `createDesktopTable`, and its expand column sticks with the other leading columns while columns are pinned. In every kit's desktop table, rails and headers carry `data-pinned` while they stick, header cells `data-sticky` while the header sticks, column headers `data-sorted`, and virtual spacer rows the `virtual-spacer` part. A kit's desktop paint can leave selected and matched cells to its classes (`classHighlight`), and `usePartClass` (`KitUsePartClass`) gives the host's class to the parts the binding draws itself; `createMobileCards` takes it too, along with an extra row's and a group card's list-item props (`extraProps`, `groupProps`) and the list's `dir`. The unstyled phone cards come from `createMobileCards`. Its group rows and cards come from `createGroupHeader`; a group footer or "show more" card lines its label up with the group's label, after the toggle's space. Every kit's Columns menu names its panel `column-menu-panel`, its title, each item (the reorder and actions rows too, as `data-reorder` and `data-actions` items) with its grip, visibility, label, pin and more controls, the rule, and the size and reset buttons; visibility and pin carry `data-active` while on, pins state `aria-pressed`, and a hidden column's label carries `data-hidden`. `KitColumnMenuLook` takes `flatItem` and `footerDivider`, and `createColumnMenu` and `createColumnHeaderRename` take `usePartClass`. The unstyled Columns menu and header rename come from the binding. Every kit's saved-views button carries `data-active` while open and its rule is `views-divider`; `createSavedViewsMenu` takes `usePartClass`, and the unstyled saved-views menu comes from it. The unstyled pager comes from `createPager` and `createPageButtons`. `KitTableClassNames` takes the host's `tableFooter`, the frame receives the table's `density`, the skeleton renderer a `KitSkeletonShape`, and the toolbar `loadsMore`; the unstyled table is laid out by `TableComposition`. Rows stagger in again only when the table holds a different number of rows, not when a regroup or a move redraws them. `KitColumnMenuLook` takes `panelInset`, the room a kit's popover adds around the panel. antd's Columns menu, header rename and saved-views menu come from the binding; its saved-views menu carries the `views-*` parts. antd's table footer comes from `createPager` with antd's pagination. The React kits no longer list `@adapttable/core` as a dependency; their types name `@adapttable/react`, which declares `BatchRowEdit` itself. Every data and summary cell carries the column's cell attributes, and the expand column's header is named for screen readers.
