What a turn did is shown on request. The reply leads, and one control under it
opens the actions and closes them again — the count names only what a reader
would want shown, so a turn that reads rows and sorts says one action, not two.

The assistant answers in its own words instead of offering a menu. Putting a
question to the reader is now a last resort for something only they can
answer, not a way to confirm or to check it understood.

A host's own capability says what it is in its receipt and in the standing
permissions, instead of showing the reader a key like `staff.raiseTeam`. A turn
that stopped short says what that means for the person who asked. Messages from
the same speaker group under one mark.
